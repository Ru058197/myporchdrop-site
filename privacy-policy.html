Privacy Policy

PorchDrop · myporchdrop.com

Last updated: June 28, 2026. This policy explains what information PorchDrop collects when you visit our site or join our waitlist, and how we use it.

1. Who we are

PorchDrop ("we," "us," "our") operates the website located at myporchdrop.com and any related landing or campaign pages. This policy applies to information collected through those pages.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Information you give us directly: your email address when you join our waitlist or newsletter, and any information you include when you contact us directly (such as via email or social media).
  • Information collected automatically: standard technical data such as your browser type, device type, general location (city/region level), and pages visited, typically collected through analytics or hosting tools.

We do not collect payment information directly on this site. Any future Kickstarter pledges or direct purchases are processed by third-party platforms (such as Kickstarter or a payment processor), governed by their own privacy policies.

3. How we use your information

  • To send you updates about PorchDrop, including launch announcements, restock notices, and campaign news, if you've opted in.
  • To respond to questions or messages you send us.
  • To understand how people use our site, so we can improve it.
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Email and marketing communications

If you provide your email address to join our waitlist or newsletter, we use a third-party email service provider (such as Mailchimp) to store your email and send communications. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out at any time.

5. Third-party services

We may use third-party tools for hosting, analytics, and email delivery. These providers may process limited technical information (such as IP address) as part of operating their services, under their own privacy policies.

6. Cookies

Our site may use basic cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences and understand site usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Data retention

We retain your email address for as long as you remain subscribed, or until you ask us to delete it.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us using the information below. You can also unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in any message we send.

9. Children's privacy

Our site and products are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to us through the contact links in our site footer.
