Before you ask

Quick answers.

Will drivers actually use it?

Yep. They spend all day looking for somewhere obvious to set things down, and this is exactly that. We're showing real Ring footage instead of a 3D render because the raised "PLACE FOOD HERE" marker speaks for itself in the ten seconds a driver spends at your door.

Why not just use a side table I already own?

You can, if you enjoy watching it warp by August. Most porch furniture isn't built to live outside year-round, sit at the right drop height, or survive 75 lbs of someone's entire Costco order. PorchDrop is purpose-built for exactly this one job.

What's it made of?

A weatherproof, easy-clean material chosen specifically to hold up to sun, rain, and daily use without breaking down. Not your average plastic patio table, and it won't pretend to be one.

Is this just for food delivery?

No, it's just as happy holding Amazon boxes, groceries, and anything else that currently lands on your porch floor. Leave it out and it quietly becomes the default drop spot for everything.
